Why our cache kept serving stale data

Problem

Users saw old prices for minutes after an update, even though the cache TTL was only 30 seconds.

What we learned

A second cache layer in the CDN was ignoring our headers and holding responses for 10 minutes.

Takeaways

  • Map every cache layer before tuning one
  • Invalidate on write, don't rely on TTL alone
